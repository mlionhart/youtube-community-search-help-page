What’s new in YouTube Community Search

This update makes Live Sync much richer — without wiping the posts you already saved.

Live Sync can now bring in

  • Poll options and votes
  • Post images
  • Posts beyond the old ~500 export cap (when Studio returns them)
  • Better merge: Get latest adds and updates by Post ID — it does not replace your whole library

Zip upload (advanced)

Studio zip/CSV is still available under Sync, but it is limited: metrics and text only — not full poll layouts or images. Prefer Live Sync (Get posts / Get latest posts) when you can.

Window modes

In Settings you can choose how the extension opens: toolbar popup, detached window, or side panel. Finish after a sync opens your preferred shell.

Free vs Pro

Free shows your top posts by impressions (up to 500). Pro unlocks the full library, advanced analytics, CSV export, and multiple channels. Existing free users keep their history; nothing is deleted to enforce the display cap.
