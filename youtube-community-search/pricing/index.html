YouTube Community Search — Product & pricing

Last updated: August 7, 2026

What it is

YouTube Community Search is a Chrome extension for YouTube creators and channel managers. It imports your YouTube Community posts from YouTube Studio Analytics (Live Sync), stores them locally in your browser, and lets you search, filter, and review performance. An optional Studio zip import is available as a backup for text and metrics.

Install the free extension from the Chrome Web Store. Pro is an optional paid upgrade inside the app.

Who it’s for

Creators and social/community managers who post in YouTube Community and want a searchable local library plus clearer post insights — without sending post content to the developer’s servers for normal use.

Free vs Pro

Free includes:

  • Live Sync / Get posts from YouTube Studio Analytics
  • Local search, filters of your entire library of posts basic post / channel analytics
  • Display of your top posts by impressions (up to 500 shown; full sync data can still be stored locally)
  • Basic post / channel analytics

Pro unlocks:

  • Full library display (no 500-post display cap)
  • Advanced analytics
  • Best time to post insights
  • Blueprints (formats of your top posts)
  • CSV export
  • Multiple channels (up to 3)

Pricing

  • Pro Monthly — $7.97 per month (subscription)
  • Pro Yearly — $71.64 per year ($5.97/mo, billed yearly)
  • Founding Member yearly — $50 per year when a limited-time founding offer is shown in the extension after you first open Upgrade
  • 7-day free trial — available on selected plans in the extension (cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged)

Prices are in USD. Applicable taxes may be added at checkout depending on your location.

How upgrade works

  1. Open the extension → &+2630 → Upgrade.
  2. Choose a trial or paid plan. Secure checkout opens in your browser.
  3. After checkout, paste your license key on the Upgrade page to unlock Pro on that install. You will have 5 uses per license key.

You can manage or cancel a subscription from the receipt / customer portal email you get after purchase. For product help, email mike@lionhartweb.com.

This doesn’t replace canceling a subscription if you want to stop future renewals — cancel anytime from your billing portal so you aren’t charged again.
